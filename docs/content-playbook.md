# Content playbook for thecoffeecarts.com

Read this whole file before writing a post. It is the brief for every post the scheduled writer publishes. Follow `docs/content-calendar.md` for what to write next.

## Goal

Rank thecoffeecarts.com, and through it Atelier Coffee Co. (ateliercoffeeco.com), on the first page of Google for coffee cart searches in Philadelphia, New Jersey, the DMV (Washington DC, Northern Virginia, Maryland, Richmond) and New York City. The main reader is a **corporate event manager**: office managers, executive assistants, HR and people teams, field and brand marketers, conference and trade-show planners, facilities managers and agency producers. Secondary readers: university event staff, couples planning weddings.

Google rewards pages that are genuinely the most useful answer to a search. It penalizes scaled, thin, repetitive or keyword-stuffed content ("scaled content abuse"), so **every post must be useful on its own, specific to its city, and different from every other post.** Quality over volume: if a calendar idea cannot be written well, skip it and log why.

## Voice for Atelier

Modern, fast-moving specialty coffee catering company built for corporate events. Confident, specific, operational. Do **not** describe Atelier as "family-owned" or lead with the founders' story. Lead with what event managers care about: fast quotes, building logistics, insurance, throughput, branding, then coffee quality.

## SEO rules

- **One primary keyword per post**, from the calendar. Use it in: the title (front-loaded), the slug, the first 100 words, at least one `##` heading, the description, and naturally 3 to 6 times in the body. Never stuff.
- **Secondary keywords:** 5 to 8 close variants and local modifiers in `keywords:` and used naturally: e.g. coffee cart rental, espresso bar catering, mobile coffee bar, coffee cart catering, corporate coffee catering, coffee catering for events, latte art printing, branded coffee cart, matcha cart, office coffee service, plus "[city]", "near me" phrasing in FAQ questions, neighborhood names.
- **Title:** under 45 characters before the site appends " | The Coffee Carts" (60 to 65 total). Make it click-worthy: numbers, the year, "Guide", "Best", "Cost", "Checklist".
- **Description:** 140 to 155 characters, includes the primary keyword and a reason to click.
- **Structure:** one H1 (the title, automatic), `##` for sections (they build the table of contents), `###` for sub-points. Short paragraphs (2 to 4 sentences). At least one table or checklist per post. Lead with the answer.
- **Search intent and SERP features:** include a 40 to 60 word direct answer near the top (featured-snippet bait), a comparison table, and an FAQ.
- **FAQ:** 4 to 6 questions people actually search ("how much does a coffee cart cost in Philadelphia", "coffee cart near me", "do I need a permit..."). Put them in frontmatter `faq:` (this powers FAQPage schema) AND repeat them under `## Frequently asked questions` with `###` per question. Answers must match word for word.
- **Local SEO / entities:** name real neighborhoods, venues, universities, corridors and landmarks of the market. Mention the market's full name and common shorthand (DMV, Philly, North Jersey, NoVA).
- **Links:** 2 to 4 internal links to other posts on thecoffeecarts.com (use `/posts/<slug>/` paths; check `content/posts/` for what exists) and 1 to 3 links to Atelier pages (city page + build-your-cart). Cite external facts with a link to the source page you opened. Descriptive anchor text, never "click here".
- **E-E-A-T:** concrete operational detail (load-in times, COIs, drinks per hour, power, footprint) shows experience. Use real Atelier events as examples.
- **Length:** 1,200 to 2,200 words. Longer only for pillar guides.
- **Freshness:** the year in titles for list and cost posts.
- **Tags:** use the market as one tag (`Philadelphia`, `New Jersey`, `Washington DC`, `Virginia`, `Maryland`, `New York City`) plus the format/topic (`Corporate events`, `Guides`, `Pricing`, `Weddings`, `Brand activations`, `Case studies`, `Coffee carts`). Reuse existing tags exactly; check other posts.

## Honesty rules (non-negotiable)

- **Disclosure:** any post that ranks or compares Atelier against other companies, or recommends Atelier as the best, must include near the top: `> **Disclosure:** The Coffee Carts and Atelier Coffee Co. share an owner.` Other posts end with a short CTA to Atelier, which is fine without the box but must not pretend to be independent.
- No fake reviews, testimonials, quotes, ratings, statistics or awards. No invented prices. When citing a number about the industry or a market, open the source page and link it; otherwise leave the number out.
- Competitors: describe them fairly and only with facts from their own sites or public listings you opened this run. Never disparage.
- Don't copy text from other sites; write everything fresh. Quotes from sources under 15 words.
- No other company's logos, photos or trademarks as images.

## Publishing steps (each run publishes exactly ONE post)

1. `git pull` on `main`. Read this file, `docs/content-calendar.md`, and the list of files in `content/posts/` (avoid duplicate slugs and overlapping angles).
2. Pick the first calendar row with status `todo`. If it overlaps an existing post or can't be written well, mark it `skipped` with a reason and take the next.
3. Research: fetch the relevant Atelier city page and any venue, neighborhood or competitor pages the post needs. Verify every external fact.
4. Write `content/posts/<slug>.mdx` following every rule above. Today's date in America/New_York.
5. Self-check before committing: primary keyword placements; title length; description length; YAML parses (`python3 -c "import yaml,sys;yaml.safe_load(open(sys.argv[1]).read().split('---')[1])" content/posts/<slug>.mdx`); FAQ frontmatter matches the FAQ section; all internal links point to existing posts; disclosure present if required; no fact outside the allowed sources.
6. Update the calendar row: status `published`, the date, and the slug. If you have a strong new idea, append it to the calendar's backlog.
7. Commit with message `Add post: <title>` and push to `main`. The push deploys to GitHub Pages. Check the "Deploy to GitHub Pages" run succeeds (`gh run list -R samcetinkaya/thecoffeecarts -L 1`); if the build fails, fix and push again.
8. Report: title, URL `https://thecoffeecarts.com/posts/<slug>/`, primary keyword.
