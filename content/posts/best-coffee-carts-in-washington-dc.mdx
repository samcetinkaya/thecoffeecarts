## 1. Atelier Coffee Co.: best for DC corporate and government events

[Atelier Coffee Co.](https://ateliercoffeeco.com/coffee-cart-catering-washington-dc) is a specialty coffee catering company, launched in 2024, built for corporate event managers. In DC, that means planning around security desks and early agendas as carefully as the menu.

**What sets it apart in the District:**

- **Secure venues.** Atelier is insured, provides certificates of insurance to venue specs, and handles clearance paperwork and staff manifests for federal buildings and secure venues with enough lead time.
- **Early starts.** For an 8am service, the crew is usually on site by 6:30.
- **Tight buildings.** The compact, collapsible cart fits Georgetown townhouse corridors, hotel ballrooms with tight load-ins and rooftops, and can often ride a passenger elevator when freight isn't available.
- **Quotes in one business day,** or a quick heads up if the date is booked. No fixed packages.
- **Scale.** Each cart makes 65 to 75 specialty drinks an hour. At DC Fintech Week at National Landing in Arlington, in 2025 and 2026, Atelier ran three branded carts, each with its own barista and menu, with the sponsor's logo printed on foam.

**Menu:** espresso drinks and cold brew on small-batch roasted beans, homemade syrups such as Honey Lavender and Spiced Brown Sugar, Rocky's Matcha ceremonial-grade matcha, and Türk kahvesi, Turkish coffee brewed in a cezve, which suits embassy receptions.

**Branding:** logo bars, full cart wraps with custom menus, branded cups and sleeves, logos printed on foam and signature drinks. In Georgetown, Atelier set up branded carts on the Arc'teryx shop floor in December 2025, one in a tan Arc'teryx wrap, and ran a Madewell storefront pop-up in March 2026 with strawberry matcha and blueberry vanilla lattes.

**Best for:** client days on K Street, association breakfasts, secure-building events, embassy receptions, campus recruiting and brand activations. Request a quote through [Build your cart](https://ateliercoffeeco.com/build-your-cart).

## 2. Agape Coffee Co.

[Agape Coffee Co.](https://www.agapecoffeeco.com/) is a locally owned mobile coffee business based in Gaithersburg, Maryland, and woman-owned. It serves DC, Maryland and Northern Virginia, naming areas such as Potomac, Rockville, Bethesda, Frederick, Arlington, Alexandria, Fairfax and McLean.

The menu covers espresso drinks and signature lattes, hot or iced, with house-made syrups, local honey and coffee from local Montgomery County roasters, plus cold brew on tap and a seasonal hot chocolate bar. Its cart works indoors or outdoors, and it offers custom menus and branding on the cups, the cart and the latte art. Agape lists corporate offices, conferences, expos, trade shows, staff appreciation and brand activations alongside weddings and showers.

**Best for:** Montgomery County and Maryland corporate events, and teams that like a local-roaster story.

## 3. Scoffee

[Scoffee](https://scoffeecatering.com/) is a mobile espresso bar based in Alexandria, Virginia, serving Northern Virginia and the DMV, including Arlington, Fairfax, Falls Church, McLean, Tysons, Vienna, Reston, Washington DC, Bethesda and Chevy Chase.

Its menu includes lattes, cappuccinos, americanos, espresso and chai, with a signature Vanilla Cinnamon Latte and custom signature drinks available. Scoffee offers corporate and recurring packages, and branding options such as branded carts, custom cups, signage and custom menus. The cart needs a space of about 6 by 6 feet and its own circuit.

**Best for:** Northern Virginia offices, especially for recurring coffee service.

## 4. Loomie Coffee

[Loomie Coffee](https://loomiecoffee.com/) is a DMV coffee caterer, started in 2026, that serves DC, Northern Virginia and Maryland, including Arlington neighborhoods from Rosslyn and Ballston to Crystal City and National Landing.

The menu goes beyond espresso: in-house cold brew, cold foam, flavors made with organic cane sugar, hand-whisked ceremonial-grade matcha, hojicha and a hot chocolate bar, with oat and almond milk standard. Loomie runs a styled cart with a commercial espresso machine and two baristas, offers logo cups, custom menus and full cart branding, and says it provides certificates of insurance to venues. It also offers recurring office coffee.

**Best for:** Arlington and National Landing office events and menus with a strong matcha and tea side.

## 5. La Dolce Cup

[La Dolce Cup](https://ladolcecup.com/) calls itself luxury Italian mobile coffee catering, with minimalist carts inspired by the Amalfi Coast. It serves Washington DC and Northern Virginia.

Its menu leans on specialty lattes, such as a tiramisu latte and a brown sugar latte, with homemade syrups, matcha in several fruit flavors, Italian hot chocolate and lemonade. La Dolce Cup also offers bottled iced-latte catering that doesn't need a cart. Custom carts and branded cups are available with two to four weeks' notice, and its event list covers office events, conferences, trade shows, brand activations, weddings and school events.

**Best for:** events that want an Italian café look and a dessert-style latte menu.

## How we picked the best coffee carts in DC

We read each caterer's own site and compared them on:

1. **Coffee and menu:** bean sourcing, espresso range and non-coffee options.
2. **Branding:** wraps, branded cups and latte art.
3. **DMV coverage:** how much of DC, Northern Virginia and Maryland each serves.
4. **DC logistics:** insurance certificates, secure-building paperwork and load-in.
5. **Scale:** how each staffs larger events.
6. **Corporate fit:** quote process and corporate offerings.

We left out reviews and ratings, and we did not compare prices because most of these caterers quote each event.

## DC booking checklist

Whichever caterer you choose, ask these before you sign:

- Can you issue a certificate of insurance with our building's additional-insured language?
- Can you provide a staff manifest or complete our vendor clearance?
- What time will the crew arrive, and how long is setup?
- Does the cart fit our elevator and entrance?
- How many drinks per hour does one cart make, and when should we add a second?
- What branding can you do, and what is the artwork lead time?

### Can a coffee cart come into a federal building or secure office in DC?

Often, with lead time. Ask the caterer whether it provides certificates of insurance and staff manifests. Atelier Coffee Co. handles clearance paperwork and staff manifests for federal buildings and secure venues.

### How much does a coffee cart cost in DC?

Four to six weeks works for most events, and more for secure buildings that need paperwork. May, June, September, October and December fill fastest.
