Coffee cart catering in Maryland covers a state that does very different kinds of business in places an hour apart. A biotech all-hands in Rockville, a client day in a Baltimore tower, an association breakfast at National Harbor and a waterfront reception in Annapolis share a state line and not much else. This guide splits Maryland into its event markets and tells you what to sort out before you book a cart in each.

**The short answer:** to book coffee cart catering in Maryland, send your date, building or venue, guest count, service hours and any campus security rules four to six weeks ahead. One cart makes 65 to 75 specialty drinks an hour, so plan a second barista or a second cart above roughly 150 guests. Power, water and access paperwork are all solvable when raised at first contact.

## Coffee cart catering in Maryland's life sciences corridor

The I-270 corridor is the reason Maryland deserves its own coffee cart plan. Maryland's Department of Commerce says the state has more than 1,800 life sciences companies and is home to the FDA and NIH, in its [life sciences industry overview](https://business.maryland.gov/key-industries/life-sciences/). Montgomery County alone counts more than 350 life sciences companies and names Rockville, Gaithersburg and Germantown as part of its I-270 technology corridor, according to a [paid article by Montgomery County Economic Development](https://www.areadevelopment.com/ContributedContent/q2-2026/in-montgomery-county-maryland-companies-are-shaping-the-next-generation-of-discoveries.shtml) in Area Development.

### What changes when the venue is a lab building

Life sciences buildings are not ordinary offices. Expect a few extra questions from facilities and EHS teams:

- **Where food and drink are allowed.** Lab floors are usually off limits, so the cart goes in a lobby, cafeteria, atrium or conference level.
- **Who is on site.** Many campuses want vendor staff names ahead of time, and some want them escorted.
- **Insurance.** Property managers will ask for a certificate of insurance, often with additional-insured wording.

Atelier is fully insured, issues COIs to venue specs including additional-insured language, and handles vendor registration, clearance paperwork and staff manifests for corporate campuses, federal buildings and secure venues, given lead time. If your event touches a federal campus, mention it in your first message.

## Coffee carts for conferences at National Harbor

National Harbor in Prince George's County is the DMV's big resort-conference destination. The Gaylord National Resort and Convention Center lists 543,889 square feet of total event space, the Prince George's Exhibition Hall at about 178,787 square feet, and 93 event rooms on its [events page](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/wasgn-gaylord-national-resort-and-convention-center/events).

At that scale, coffee planning is about lines, not just drinks. A sponsor lounge or exhibitor booth with a cart works best when you:

1. **Check the venue's vendor and catering policy first.** Large convention hotels often have exclusive catering rules for food and beverage, so get written approval for an outside coffee vendor before you sign anything.
2. **Size for the break, not the day.** Most conference coffee demand lands in the 30 minutes before the first session and in mid-morning breaks.
3. **Split the line.** At DC Fintech Week at National Landing in Arlington in 2025 and 2026, Atelier ran three branded carts, each with its own barista and menu, with the sponsor's logo printed on the foam. Three short lines move faster than one long one.

## Coffee cart catering in Baltimore

Baltimore has its own downtown office market, large hospital and university campuses, and a convention center in the central business district. Visit Baltimore lists 300,000 square feet of contiguous exhibition space, 85,000 square feet of meeting space across 50 rooms and a 36,672-square-foot ballroom at the [Baltimore Convention Center](https://baltimore.org/meetings/baltimore-convention-center/), which sits steps from the Inner Harbor.

For office events in Harbor East, Harbor Point or the downtown towers, the logistics look like any big-city building:

- Confirm where the van can stop and for how long; downtown curbs fill early.
- Ask which elevator the crew uses. Atelier's compact, collapsible cart fits a standard passenger elevator, so a building without a freight elevator or loading dock is fine.
- Send the building's COI requirements with the booking.

Hospital and university events (donor receptions, symposiums, staff appreciation days, match day) follow the same rules as lab buildings: food and drink zones, vendor badges and early security sign-off.

## Annapolis, Columbia and the Bay

Annapolis brings association meetings, state-government adjacent events and waterfront receptions. Columbia and Howard County host corporate parks between DC and Baltimore that draw teams from both cities. The Eastern Shore is mostly weddings and retreats.

These events tend to be outdoors or in historic buildings, so the questions are practical: what surface the cart stands on, where the nearest outlet is, whether there's shade, and what happens if it rains. Limited power is usually workable with a power plan agreed in advance, and Atelier brings its own water.

## Maryland coffee cart checklist for event managers

Copy this into your planning doc before you request quotes.

- [ ] Date, start time and service hours (most events run 2 to 4 hours)
- [ ] Venue address and the room or floor where the cart will stand
- [ ] Guest count and your busiest 30 to 45 minutes
- [ ] Building or campus security steps: vendor registration, staff manifest, escort, badges
- [ ] COI requirements, additional-insured wording and certificate holder name
- [ ] Load-in route: loading dock or street, elevator, any stairs
- [ ] An 8 by 8 foot space with a dedicated outlet if possible
- [ ] Convention venue approval for outside coffee service, if relevant
- [ ] Menu needs: dairy alternatives, decaf, matcha, non-coffee options
- [ ] Branding: logo on the bar, cart wrap, cups, sleeves, logo on foam, signature drink
- [ ] On-site contact name and phone number
- [ ] Your budget, if you have one
