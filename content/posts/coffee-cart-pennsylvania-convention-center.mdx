For trade show booth coffee, Philadelphia almost always means one building: the Pennsylvania Convention Center at 1101 Arch Street, between Chinatown and City Hall. With seven exhibit halls covering 679,000 square feet, according to the center's [by-the-numbers page](https://paconvention.com/meeting-professionals/floor-plans/by-the-numbers), it hosts medical congresses, industry expos and consumer shows all year. If you are an exhibitor, coffee is one of the cheapest ways to stop people in the aisle. It is also one of the easiest things to get wrong, because the building decides who pours.

**The short answer:** Aramark is the exclusive caterer at the Pennsylvania Convention Center. Order booth coffee, including espresso and barista service, from Aramark at least 20 business days before the show, and order your own power and counter separately. An outside espresso cart needs Aramark's written approval and waiver or corkage fees. Branded carts are simplest at the events you host around the show.

## Trade show booth coffee Philadelphia rules: who pours

The center's [exhibitor services page](https://paconvention.com/exhibitors/exhibitor-services) names Aramark as the exclusive provider of food and beverage services. Aramark's current [exhibitor menu for the center](https://www.paconvention.com/assets/doc/Exhibitor-Menu-2026-10-b504842520.pdf) spells out what that means on the floor:

- No food or beverage, down to candy and logo water, comes into the building without Aramark's approval and the appropriate waiver or corkage fees.
- Sampling guidelines and corkage terms come from an Aramark sales manager, so ask early if you plan to hand out anything you brought.
- Aramark offers "Espresso Service, Specialty Coffee, Tea, & Barista Service" on request, alongside brewed coffee, tea and decaf by the gallon, a Keurig setup and Saxbys cold brew cans.
- Food and beverage pricing carries a 24% administrative fee plus 8% tax, and full payment is due before service starts.

Menus and fees change, and your show may add its own rules, so use this as a map, not a quote. The exhibitor service kit for your show and Aramark's sales team (aramark-sales@paconvention.com) have the final word.

## Pick your booth coffee model

Exhibitors in Philly usually land on one of four setups. The right one depends on how much of your booth story rides on the coffee.

| Model | Who pours | Best for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brewed coffee by the gallon | Aramark delivers; your staff hand it out | Small booths, long show days, low budget | Little brand moment; you still need a counter and cups |
| Keurig in the booth | Your staff | Meeting-room-style booths with seating | Needs its own dedicated 2,000-watt service |
| Aramark espresso and barista service | Aramark baristas | Booths that want lattes without outside approvals | Order lead time; you still supply power and a counter |
| Approved outside espresso cart | Your chosen caterer | Wrapped carts, signature drinks, logo foam | Written approval, waiver or corkage, insurance, floor space |

If you only need people to linger at a demo station, the first two can be enough. If the coffee is the activation, with a custom menu, logo cups and a line that becomes your lead-capture queue, you are choosing between Aramark's barista service and an approved outside cart. Ask Aramark first; what its team can do in your booth may already cover the brief for your trade show booth coffee in Philadelphia.

## The exhibitor timeline

Aramark's deadlines arrive earlier than most exhibitors expect, and power and furniture run on separate orders. Work backward from the first show day:

| When | Task | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| As soon as booth space is confirmed | Read your show's exhibitor kit, including food and beverage and sampling rules | Your show |
| 6 to 8 weeks out | If you want an outside cart, email Aramark sales and show management together and get the answer in writing | Our recommendation |
| 4 to 6 weeks out | Book your coffee vendor (outside carts in May, June, September, October and December fill fastest) | Atelier |
| 20 business days out | Original Aramark catering orders due, or a 10% late charge applies | Aramark menu |
| Same window | Order electrical from PCC Exhibitor Services and a counter or skirted table from your show contractor | Center and Aramark menu |
| 3 business days out | Last changes or cancellations to the Aramark order (later changes carry a 20% fee and need approval) | Aramark menu |
| Show day | Schedule any Aramark staff to arrive an hour before service starts, as the menu recommends | Aramark menu |

Show managers have their own clock too. The center's [plan of operations](https://www.paconvention.com/assets/doc/Plan-of-Ops-July-2026-v.1-e278605816.pdf) asks for preliminary floor plans at least 90 days before the first move-in day for fire marshal review, so a large outside coffee bar or sponsor lounge should be on the show's radar well before your catering deadline.

## Power, counters and the fire marshal

Coffee equipment is a power problem first. Aramark's menu is direct about it: it does not provide skirted service tables or electrical hookups in your exhibit space, and items such as the Keurig and water cooler need a dedicated 2,000-watt service that the client orders.

### Order power through Exhibitor Services

Electrical, plumbing and compressed air at the center are ordered through PCC Exhibitor Services (215-418-2190, exhibitorservices@paconvention.com), per the [exhibitor services page](https://paconvention.com/exhibitors/exhibitor-services). For an espresso setup, ask your vendor for the exact power draw and give it to Exhibitor Services rather than guessing. A dedicated outlet per cart is ideal. Atelier's cart brings its own water, so a plumbing order is usually not needed for an approved Atelier cart.

### Ask about heat-producing equipment

The plan of operations says the center requires prior written authorization for heaters, heat-producing or open-flame devices, and compressed gases, and that fuel storage in the building is prohibited by order of the Philadelphia Fire Department. An espresso machine is electric, not open flame, but ask your Event Manager whether your setup needs sign-off. A one-line email now beats a conversation with the fire marshal on move-in day.

### Plan the footprint

Atelier's cart needs about 8 x 8 feet including barista space. In a 10 x 10 booth that is nearly the whole booth, which leaves no room for the queue or your demo. If coffee is the draw, a 10 x 20 or larger footprint gives you one zone for the cart, one for the line, and one for the conversation that follows the drink.

## Getting into the building

The center does not accept event freight; it goes to the show's official contractor or to show management, according to the plan of operations. The same document lists the delivery points:

- **Docks A to E, Grand Hall and Ballroom:** Vine Street ramp (GPS 1130 Vine Street)
- **Dock F:** 1140 Race Street
- **Dock G:** 151 North 13th Street
- **Truck marshaling yard:** 7th and Callowhill, for vehicles only

The center also says temporary show labor is provided by Elliott Lewis. A [2019 BizBash report](https://www.bizbash.com/corporate-events/pennsylvania-convention-center-expands-exhibitor-rights-agreement) described the center's Customer Satisfaction Agreement as letting exhibitors set up and tear down their own booths without a booth-size limit; confirm the current terms with Contractor Services (215-418-4900) before you plan who carries what.

For an approved outside cart, Atelier's compact, collapsible design fits a standard passenger elevator, which helps when freight routes are jammed during move-in. Atelier also handles certificates of insurance to venue specs, including additional-insured language, and vendor paperwork for secure venues with enough lead time.

## How much coffee one booth actually pours

Booth coffee is different from a conference break. You are not serving the whole show, only the people who walk into your space, and traffic follows the show schedule: heavy right after doors open, quieter during keynotes, a second surge after lunch.

Each Atelier cart makes 65 to 75 specialty drinks an hour. Here is what that looks like over a show day:

| Service pattern | Hours of pouring | Drinks per cart (estimate) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morning only, doors open to lunch | 3 | About 195 to 225 |
| Two peaks, morning and post-lunch | 2 + 2 | About 260 to 300 |
| A seven-hour show day, start to finish | 7 | About 455 to 525 |

These are planning estimates, not promises. If your goal is 200 badge scans a day, a single cart through the morning peak is in the right range. If you need more, add a second barista or a second cart before you stretch the hours, because the morning rush is where the line forms. For a deeper look at running several stations side by side, read our [sponsor lounge case study from DC Fintech Week](/posts/sponsor-lounge-coffee-dc-fintech-week/), where Atelier ran three branded carts, each with its own barista and menu.

## Turning the coffee line into booth traffic

The coffee is the hook; the line is the opportunity. A few tactics that work whether Aramark or an approved vendor is pouring:

- **Scan while they wait.** Put a staffer with the badge scanner at the end of the line, not at the cart. Two minutes in line is a natural opening for a question.
- **Make the cup carry the brand.** Branded cups or printed sleeves keep your logo moving through the aisles after the guest leaves.
- **Print the logo on the foam.** With an approved outside cart, Atelier's latte art printer puts your logo on each drink, a detail made for the photo people post from the show.
- **Name a signature drink after your product.** Atelier builds them on homemade syrups such as Honey Lavender, Butter Brown Sugar and Spiced Brown Sugar.
- **Offer more than coffee.** Matcha, tea or a hibiscus refresher keeps the line open to guests who do not want another espresso.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who provides catering at the Pennsylvania Convention Center?

Aramark is the exclusive food and beverage provider at the Pennsylvania Convention Center. Booth coffee, from brewed coffee by the gallon to espresso and barista service, is ordered through Aramark's exhibitor catering team at aramark-sales@paconvention.com.

### Can I bring my own coffee cart into a booth at the Pennsylvania Convention Center?

Not without approval. Aramark's exhibitor menu says no food or beverage is allowed in the building without Aramark's approval and waiver or corkage fees. Ask Aramark's sales team and your show manager in writing before you book an outside vendor.

### When do I need to order booth coffee for a Philadelphia trade show?

Aramark's exhibitor menu asks for original catering orders 20 business days before the first show day, or a 10% late charge applies. Changes and cancellations are due 3 business days before the first show day.

### Does Aramark provide power and a table for coffee in my booth?

No. Aramark's exhibitor menu says it does not provide skirted service tables or electrical hookups. Order power from PCC Exhibitor Services and your counter or table from the show's contractor.

### Is there trade show booth coffee near me in Philadelphia outside the convention center?

Yes. For events you host around the show, such as a Center City office breakfast or a client reception, Atelier Coffee Co. brings a branded espresso cart, handles the COI and sends a custom quote within one business day.
