A coffee cart is a full espresso bar that fits in the corner of a room. It rolls in, plugs in, and an hour later there is a line of people holding flat whites. This blog is about how that works, and how to tell a good one from a forgettable one.

## What we cover

- **Booking a cart.** What to ask before you sign, how much space and power a cart needs, and how many drinks an hour to plan for.
- **Pricing.** What goes into a quote, and why two carts that look the same can cost very different amounts.
- **Running a cart.** Equipment, menus, staffing and the logistics nobody sees.

## Who it is for

Anyone planning an event who wants better coffee than an urn on a folding table, and anyone who runs a cart or is thinking about starting one.
